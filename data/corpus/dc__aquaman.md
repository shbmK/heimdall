# Orin (New Earth)

A king is a king in every corner of his kingdom... and when he's not in his kingdom? He's still a king. 
— Marlus Randone

Aquaman is the ruler of Atlantis and the Earth's oceans, an Atlantean with incredible strength and speed as well as the ability to command all sea-life. His unique physiology allows him to survive on land and at the ocean's greatest depths of pressure and temperature. Given the names Orin through his royal heritage and Arthur Curry by his human upbringing, he fights to protect both worlds using his mighty abilities and political influence. He is also a founding member of the Justice League of America.

## History

### Origins

Main article: Aquaman Origins

Originally in Silver Age continuity Arthur Curry was the son of a lighthouse keeper named Tom Curry and a woman named Atlanna. Tom Curry lived in Amnesty Bay, Maine and raised his son with wife in the local Lighthouse. Arthur started to manifest incredible strength and speed at a young age, as well as the abilities to breathe underwater and talk to fish. On her deathbed, his mother revealed that she was an exiled Queen of Atlantis and he would someday inherit rule of the Seven Seas. His father gave him an education and taught him to control his powers, eventually allowing him to grow up and become the super-hero Aquaman.

His origins were changed when the universe was rebooted during the Crisis on Infinite Earths. Orin was born an heir to the Atlantean Royal Family. His mother, Atlanna, had been unable to conceive a child with her husband, King Trevis, but she was impregnated during a dream by the immortal wizard Atlan. Atlan prophesied the child's future and named him after his ancestor, Orin, promising that he would sire another son because two brothers must always struggle for the fate of Atlantis. Trevis immediately knew the child was not his because of the blonde hair, a long-held superstition that a child might carry the Curse of Kordax. The baby was announced as a miscarriage and left to die on Mercy Reef, where his ability to communicate with sealife allowed him to be raised by dolphins. His adoptive mother was named Porm and she gave him the name the Swimmer, although he left this new family after fishermen killed his adopted brother, Drin.

His next encounter with humanity was a lighthouse operator named Tom Curry who took him in as a son, giving him the human name Arthur Curry before his death. Spending time in Alaska, he met and fell in love with the beautiful Inuit girl Kako. They conceived a child together, but the demonic god Nuliajuk forced Arthur to leave before he could learn of the pregnancy. His next fight against a God would be Poseidon's son, Triton, when he rescued Princess Diana from his sexual advances. Returning to Atlantis, he was arrested and sentenced to hard labor in the Aquarium prison. He found mentorship in the former professor Vulko who taught him Atlantean, although he decided to escape when he learned that his birth mother had died. Curry's first encounter with another super-hero was the Flash, who named him "Aquaman" during a press conference in Crescent Shore after they fought the Trickster. Upon his next return, Orin learned that Vulko had led a revolution and claimed his birthright as the King of Atlantis.

This has supposedly changed during Infinite Crisis, as a conversation with Jackson Hyde during Brightest Day revealed him to be once again the son of Atlanna and lighthouse keeper Tom Curry.

### The Justice League

Main article: Justice League Origins

Aquaman would go on to become a founding member of the Justice League of America, although the team's origins have been altered several times in continuity. The original founding members included Batman, the Flash, Green Lantern, the Martian Manhunter, Superman and Wonder Woman. They met for the first time during an alien invasion by the Appellaxians. This was later amended Post-Crisis to write Superman and Batman out of the story, with Wonder Woman replaced by the Black Canary.

### The Aquaman Family

Main article: Aquaman Family

Arthur met a youth abandoned at birth by the Idylists and took him in as his young partner, the original Aqualad. He would later meet Mera, the Queen of Dimension Aqua. They would eventually marry. Their first child together was named Arthur Curry, Jr..

### Death of a Prince

Main article: Aquaman: Death of a Prince

Black Manta returned with a series of more vicious personal attacks, beginning with an assault on Atlantis' agricultural system. Struggling under the political responsibilities of his position, Aquaman commissioned the brilliant inventor Weisbogg to create a robotic duplicate for his ceremonial duties, although the scientist had been working for Manta and created a mechanized Bugala that almost destroyed the city. Manta would next bury him under several tons of rubble, although he escaped through a cavernous passage with the guidance of cave-dwelling fish. The next great threat would be an invasion by Captain Demo's high-tech pirates who actually managed to subjugate the city with terrorist threats until Aquaman managed to disable their explosives and defeat them. NATO came into conflict with Atlantis when the dangerous terrorist group the New Order swam through their waters carrying the stolen potential to make a nuclear warhead. General Horgan launched a missile believing that the hundreds of Atlanteans lost in the crossfire would be nothing compared to the potential thousands of dead. The missile was barely averted in time for Aquaman to take down the terrorists single-handedly and defuse their hostage situation. Passing through France, he encountered an aquatic drug-smuggling operation organized by the Fisherman using dolphins to traffic heroin, and helped Interpol shut it down. Believing him to be spending too much time away from the city, the Atlantean Royal Council held a democratic election and had him dethroned, instating a villain in disguise named Karshon in his place as king. Discovering his impeachment upon his return to Atlantis, Aquaman was framed for stealing a vial of Serum X from the royal armory during a battle against the Ocean Master to save Monaco. Karshon made him an enemy of the state, and he was forced into exile while his former subjects hunted him down across the ocean.

Running from the law, Aquaman and his family established a new Aquacave as their hideout. Arthur and Mera were kidnapped by the men of a nearby small town ruled by its high priest, Toxxin, who believed they were part of a prophecy to destroy the monster named the Krakor, although they defeated him and escaped after slaying it. Aqualad and Aquagirl returned to help solve the conspiracy when they broke up one of Black Manta's smuggling operations in Mississippi. Investigating Manta's gun-running of laser rifles to Atlantis, Aquaman was caught in one of the Fisherman's deathtraps to distract him while Karshon sent telepathically-controlled sharks to kidnap Mera and Aquababy. Aquaman was defeated by Black Manta in his rage, and imprisoned alongside Vulko as a traitor. He broke out and returned to his throne room to challenge Karshon, who was revealed to be a telepathic enemy of Green Lantern known as the Shark. They fought for their lives while Aquababy and Mera faced a deathtrap, until Aquaman tricked the Shark into overdosing on radiation and being poisoned as a result of this. Once the city was restored to sanity and order, and having decided that it was too much of a challenge trying to handle both responsibilities, he ceded the crown to Vulko in his absence and decided to become a super-hero full-time.

His next adventure was to help Horgan save a boatload of swine flu vaccines from the telepathic Marine Marauder. He helped NATO again when the Weather Wizard stole an experimental medical device being transported across the Atlantic. Chasing after Topo to save Aqualad from trouble, he also met a lost tribe called the Idylists when he saved Sett and Thoran from an undersea Starro mutated by pollution. In the City of the Lost Tribes, he discovered that Black Manta had easily conquered the pacifistic Idylists and was attempting to set up an underwater colony for Black people to escape persecution on the surface. Aquaman and Aqualad were put in a shielded arena together and forced to fight to the death for Aquababy's life. Although they managed to break out and destroy Manta's operations, they were too late to stop him from killing the child and escaping. Aqualad decided to remain behind with the Idylists and finally discovered his parentage as the son of their king, Thar and their queen, Berra, who had been assassinated years earlier.

Seeking revenge against Black Manta, he pursued him to a sunken mine field and almost died fighting the villain; he was assisted by one of the mercenaries who grew disillusioned with the slaughter that was involved in Manta's plan, Cal Durham. In a critical moment as he was about to murder his nemesis, Aquaman remembered that his ideals needed to stand for something and had him arrested instead.

The Fisherman struck again by attempting to fry him while he was tied to the Curry Lighthouse, although Arthur escaped by remembering his mother Atlanna's words to summon strength and went after him. Defeating him in the Marianas Trench, they were both taken by surprise when the Scavenger stole the cargo that the Fisherman had been trying to steal. The trail led back to Kobra, who had been manipulating the other super-villains and stole the several tons of explosives for himself. Aquaman teamed up with Batman and Green Lantern to stop the Kobra Cult from destroying Portugal and moving on to the rest of the world. Meanwhile, in Atlantis, it turned out that Aquababy had not died from suffocation and was being kept on life support by Vulko. Mera returned to Dimension Aqua to find a machine designed by Xebel that could save him, although instead she was forced to depose the tyrannical regime of Leron established as dictator in her absence. By the time she returned it was too late, and she faced the agony of her son's death a second time. Aquaman returned in the middle of the funeral and Mera tried to kill him for not being there when their son needed him most, believing that his super-heroics and revenge were more important to him than his own family. They were interrupted by a villain named Sea Quake who almost destroyed the city with destructive sonic waves, although he was revealed to be a mindless robot. The Ocean Master had been controlling the robot to create a fissure in the ocean floor, diving down to retrieve an ancient Atlantean super-weapon. Aquaman defeated him alongside Aqualad and Mera, and the family seemed to be together again, if not entirely complete.

### New Venice

Returning to active duty, he took down a big game hunter named Colin Landau who specialized in hunting whales. Vulko made a business deal with the sinister Universal Food Products to share farming secrets, unaware of their ulterior motives. Aquaman discovered that their leader, Mr. Amos, intended to make Atlantis a desolate wasteland, but Vulko refused to believe him and had him arrested for interfering. Despite his wife and partner being sent to subdue him, he evaded the guards and proved his innocence by taking the corporation down. Atlantis was threatened again by the Greek tycoon Stavros Markos, who sent them into a blackout while his goons attempted a coup. They also fought off a Nazi contingent who attempted to steal their secrets by replacing the government with obedient holograms.

In the Bermuda Triangle, he stumbled upon the literal Graveyard of Ships, which he discovered was a dimension accidentally created by the scientist Atlena. Returning to New Venice, he learned that they had developed an Aquaman Museum and decided to make his new base there. Doctor Light was the first to discover this new home, and attempted to assassinate him.

The Scavenger attempted to steal the machinery that they needed to rescue Atlena and her people. New Venice was attacked by a Poseidon impostor who telepathically commanded an army of marine life, but he was defeated when his trident was destroyed. Cal Durham returned to ally himself with Aquaman when he got out of prison. Black Manta put together a derelict army called the Kingdom of Hope to invade Atlantis, and simultaneously launched nuclear missiles at New Venice to keep his nemesis busy; he was asked why, and said that it was simply because he hated Aquaman's guts. Knocking the missiles off-course, he ended the battle by promising the homeless soldiers that they would be given a place to live without having to fight for it.

The Ocean Master was revealed to be responsible for the Kingdom of Hope, using a Black Manta robot. He escaped capture and the Poseidon impostor attacked again. Atlanna was revealed to have survived and turned evil, working with Orm to destroy her son. Poseidon turned good when he was revealed to be an android given the personality and memories of Tom Curry. They battled, and Atlanna used a robotic army of Aquaman's greatest enemies. Atlena appeared telepathically and appealed to her conscience, leading Atlanna to once again destroy herself and her android husband. Before exploding, they asked Arthur and Orm to love each other as their dying wish. In his next adventure, the Atom helped him investigate a new undersea life form.

His next team-up was with Air Wave, when a Zeta-Beam put them into contact with extra-terrestrial marine biology. This transported him to the planet Vortuma where he met the Hexapuses, an aquatic race attempting to resist control by the tyrannical Land-Masters. He defeated them when he realizeed that they were evil Hexapuses masquerading as surface-dwellers. Exposing the traitor inside the resistance, he finally found a way back home with the help of Professor Hexapus.

Mera reappeared from her self-imposed exile into Xebel, now working as a Navy marine biologist. She had amnesia and believeed herself to be Miriam Bridgeman, but Arthur demonstrated her true identity through a series of physical tests. She was revealed to be possessed by her political rival, V'lana, who tried to strangle Aquaman. Mera was reawakened when they traveled back to her dimension, and they fought V'lana with her newly learned control over sea creatures. They defeated her, and she committed suicide rather than face failure. Now cured of her vibratory illness, Mera abdicated her throne willingly to be with the man she loved.

### Justice League Detroit

Main article: Justice League Detroit

The Justice League Satellite was destroyed during the Earth/Mars War. Mera left him in its wake, because she believed that since the death of their son, they had not been able to properly love each other. He formally disbanded the League as acting chairman at the U.N., announcing that they could not protect Earth without complete commitment from all of their members. The new team that rose in their place included Aquaman, the Elongated Man, Gypsy, the Martian Manhunter, Steel, Vibe, Vixen and Zatanna. To remain closer to those they were sworn to protect, they established a new headquarters in a Detroit compound operated by Dale Gunn.

### Crisis on Infinite Earths

Main article: Crisis on Infinite Earths

Aquaman was one of the many heroes brought together by Harbinger during the great Crisis, when realities were merging and disappearing. Atlantis disappeared in their reality; Black Manta and the Ocean Master took this opportunity to plot against him while he was weak. Aquagirl is mortally wounded at the hands of Chemo's toxic pollution. Arthur and Mera were frozen together by Captain Cold in a battle that ceded Earth-S as a territory of the super-villains. He rallied with the others at the Big Bang to fight the Anti-Monitor. Tula died of her injuries. After the dawn of existence was reset, one true Earth emerges from the erased Multiverse. Its heroes met collectively at Titans Tower in the Crisis's wake. In the Anti-Monitor's last strike before he was completely destroyed, Aquaman stood alongside his allies to protect Atlantis from the Shadow Demons. Lori Lemaris fought bravely but was killed during the battle.

### Thicker Than Water

Main article: Aquaman: Thicker Than Water

New Venice was destroyed by the Ocean Master, who returned using sorcery-related powers. Tensions surrounding Atlantean hostility towards surface dwellers led to a declaration of war after one of their Zodiac Crystals was stolen. Aquaman was forced to adopt a new blue costume to hide his identity while the people cried out for his blood, and he investigated the disappearance in Thierna Na Oge. Their society of the Tuatha de Danann believed him to be a malevolent spy, and their king, Bres, had him put to death by the Sreng in their arena. His escape was assisted by the mage Nuada Silverhand, who had also been accused of stealing a crystal. Ocean Master ambushes them at Maarzon and is revealed to have gained his powers from stealing the crystals. Nuada explains that the magics are fueled by emotion, and the only way to beat Orm's rage is for Arthur to overcome his own. They fight on the astral plane as Aquaman struggles eternally, finally destroying the crystals when he reaches an epiphany and tells Orm that he loves him. This enlightenment was short-lived however, as the battle was revealed to have torn his soul-self into two parts. The crystals were activated again by a scientist named Magus who harnessed their power to create another universe, and Aquaman was able to make himself whole by fighting inside to reunite his emotion and reason.

### Invasion!

Main article: Invasion!

During Invasion! Aquaman rode Storm to lead his armies against the Alien Alliance, fighting Gil'Dishpan at the Arctic Circle. He teams up with the Doom Patrol and the Sea Devils to destroy their command ship, delivering a devastating blow to the enemy military.

### Tide of Battle

Main article: Aquaman: Tide of Battle

Mera had been driven insane by her grief and was committed to an asylum, while Arthur's throne was turned over to the incompetent Pletus in his absence. Atlantis was conquered and enslaved by a race of Giant Jellyfish under this reign, and Aquaman returned to be imprisoned inside Aquarium again. Aquaman becomes a member of the resistance movement using violent terrorist attacks to strike back at their oppressors. The rebellion succeeds with a massive assault on the Royal Palace slaughtering every Jellyfish in the city limits. Mera escapes from her asylum and tries to murder Arthur, forcing him to seemingly kill her by accident in self-defense. She awakens from her coffin alive however and decides she's caused enough harm before returning to Dimension Aqua. Atlantis makes their last stand against the invaders during a massive siege, and Aquaman wipes out the enemy fleet by telepathically commanding an army of killer whales until he passes out. In the aftermath there is public cry for him to retake his throne, but he insists that he must be a protector of the world.

### The United Nations

Atlantis entered into a disastrous war with the surface nation of Oumland, requiring Aquaman to bail them out of trouble once again. They resolved the matter diplomatically with some difficulty, after he personally stormed their borders. Distracting him once again from his people, he helped Superman fight prehistoric monsters; Lori Lemaris was revealed to have faked her own death. The city's dome was destroyed in the battle and sharks attacked, beset by a feeding frenzy, leading the Tritonians led by Iqula to invade Poseidonis. Aquaman defeated Iqula in single combat, proving his honor. In New York City he was attacked by the fanatic Queequeg, but dispatched him with a single punch. Richard Mission approached him as a liaison to become an ambassador to the United Nations. Black Manta attacked during the press conference, then blew up his son's grave on Mercy Reef just to piss him off. Manta incapacitated his nemesis, then used fear gas to watch Poseidonis tear itself to shreds. Aquaman nearly beat Manta to death in revenge, but still could not bring himself to deliver the killing blow.

Thanatos returnd after many years to attack him through his mind again, and set up an elaborate dreamscape where he must fight his wife and dead son to survive. Aquaman refused and made peace with them instead, escaping by the only way possible. Delivering a speech in front of the U.N. on behalf of Poseidonis, Jordan Wylie hired the hitman the NKVDemon to murder him. They fought across the Manhattan rooftops in the rain, allowing Aquaman to stay on land longer than usual, and he finally defeated the assassin.

There was another diplomatic incident when the Sea Devils were involved in an oil well explosion in South America. Aquaman investigated and attempted to clean up the spill after he determined that they had been framed. He exposed the oil company's corruption to the media after the Sea Devils proved their innocence by battling a private army. Poseidonis was officially recognized by the United Nations through a democratic vote.

He fought another dinosaur that got into New York Harbor. Thesily became outraged that Aquaman kept overshadowing his public image, and recalled him to Atlantis. In a heated argument with the King, he quit his position as ambassador. Tritonis entered into a civil war between Iqula and their treacherous king, Firtf. Aquaman allied with his former rival to restore the city to peace and order in a massive battle. He later met a young boy with cancer who idolized him through the reformed Scavenger.

### Time and Tide

Main article: Aquaman: Time and Tide Vol 1

Aquaman received the Atlantis Chronicles from Vulko, who found them in the wreckage of Poseidonis. Deciding to take up his mother's legacy as chronicler, he began recording his own adventures. Discovering that his greatest nemesis the Ocean Master is really his half-brother, he began spiraling into a depression. Aqualad pulled him out of this to investigate an undersea radiation leak, but it turned out to be a trap and they were kidnapped alongside the Dolphin by the terrorist Charybdis.

The three fought their way to escape together, but Charybdis held Aquaman's hand down in a piranha pool before getting thrown in himself. Progressively nursing back to health in the Aquacave, Orin emerged with a harpoon over the stump of his left hand.

Discovering that Admiral Strom of the United States Navy was responsible for betraying him, Aquaman attacked a military base for answers and fought Superboy. Strom distracted him by revealing that Porm had been captured, and Aquaman was forced to team up with Lobo to rescue dolphins from a hunter named Tanaka in a testing facility. Returning to Alaska with the Dolphin and seeing Kako again, she introduced him to their full-grown illegitimate son, Koryak. Their village was attacked by the Deep Six from Apokolips, and Jaffar killed Kako during the raid. She was reborn through Gaia as the fire elemental Corona, but not before the Six could blight her transformation. Corona unleashed Kako's anger at Arthur for abandoning her, and the water elemental Naiad intervened to destroy her. Corona relented when Arthur agreed to let her kill him, and Koryak returned with them to Atlantis after she disappeared with Naiad. His harpoon destroyed by Corona, Aquaman had a new one designed using top-line technology at S.T.A.R. Labs. King Thesily began to view Aquaman as a threat and hired Deadline to murder him, although the assassin was defeated and escaped. Aquaman met Kyle Rayner for the first time when he was forced to defend Cron One-Eye for attacking a swimmer in self-defense. Thesily attempted to murder Koryak, but was killed by falling rubble during an earthquake.

### The Return of Thanatos

Koryak took leadership of the kingdom with Vulko, and they led the Atlanteans on a pilgrimage to Tritonis. Aquaman and the Dolphin stayed behind in Poseidonis, and Mera finally returned from her self-imposed exile to walk in on them consummating their relationship. Mera fights the Dolphin, but she was revealed to have been brainwashed by Arthur's evil doppelganger, Thanatos. Aquaman followed her into the other-dimension to fight Thanatos, and it was revealed that she had conceived another son named A.J.

The two men battled to the death as they were tested by The Others, and Thanatos was ruled to be more worthy to escape their imprisonment. Major Disaster was empowered by the demon-lord Neron during Underworld Unleashed and commissioned to kill Aquaman, murdering Thanatos instead by mistake. Atlan freed Orin and the Dolphin from the prison dimension. They returned to find a sentient starship underneath Atlantis had reawakened the dragon-like Tiamat of the Annunake from stasis, and they defeated the monster as Atlantis rose above sea level again. Koryak and Vulko's pilgrimage accidentally freed Kordax from his centuries of imprisonment. Thanatos had sent a declaration of war to the surface world before his death, and Justice League America was sent to investigate. Orin and the Dolphin fought Fire, Guy Gardner, Ice, the Martian Manhunter, Obsidian and Wonder Woman, proving their innocence as he asserted his authority.

### The Hunter/Gatherer War

Aquaman recruited the city of Hy-Brasil without too much trouble. Unfortunately, Aquaman and Dolphin encountered Ocean Master at one of the other cities, a city that was destroyed in the subsequent battle. Also during the battle, Garth returned - Aqualad no longer; he was now Tempest, having been trained by Atlan in another dimension. Aquaman then recruited the cities of Thierna Na Oge and the floating city of Bazilia. They also recruited Atlan to their cause and headed for Tritonis - all while the aliens (known as the Hunter/Gatherers) turned up in Washington D.C. under the guise of seeking peaceful relations. Aquaman found Tritonis in ruins - Koryak and Vulko had led the people of Poseidonis into the forbidden tunnels beneath the city and awakened Kordax from antiquity. Kordax, using his mind control power, forced Koryak and the citizens of Poseidonis to attack their relatives in Tritonis, maiming or killing much of the population. Aquaman helped Tritonis's people out before rallying all of his allies for the final confrontation: Atlan, the Guardian of Hy-Brasil, Arion, Tempest, Tsunami, Nuada, the Dolphin, the Sea Devils, Power Girl (at this time still thought to be a descendant of Atlantis) Spought(the Guardian of Basilia) and Deep Blue. Aquaman led the others to Washington D.C. by flying the skull ship (with Poseidonis on top) straight to the capital. A major battle ensued inside the White House with Aquaman's allies battling against Kordax, Tiamat, and Koryak (still under Kordax's control). Kordax kills himself in the battle when he could not defeat Aquaman. Basilia was destroyed in the fighting that happened there. The Hunter/Gatherers left Earth without any further destruction.

### The New World Order

During a battle against Doctor Destiny, there was a massive team-up between Aquaman, Batman, the Flash, Green Lantern, the Martian Manhunter, Superman and Wonder Woman. They decided to reform as the Justice League of America and returned to their original line-up following the dissolution of Justice League International. Their first battle together in the public eye was against Hyperclan, proving themselves to the world by stopping an invasion of White Martians.

### The Curse of Kordax

During The Final Night, the sentient starship ejected Aquaman, the Dolphin, and Koryak (now free of the brainwashing) after having a disagreement with Aquaman (regarding the amount of control the ship was exerting over Arthur). Aquaman's adoptive dolphin mother, Porm, was murdered during the darkness, crushing Aquaman. Following a clue, Aquaman ventured to Japan and discovered that the murderer of Porm was a cyborg called Demon Gate, a man who had been maimed by Lobo and who blamed Aquaman for the incident. Aquaman threatened to ban Japan from the oceans unless Demon Gate was handed over. Japan refused. Aquaman recruited the Martian Manhunter to retake Poseidonis, forcing the sentient starship to obey him, and then took the city to Japan. Demon Gate was turned over and Arthur sentenced him to life imprisonment on a deserted island, surviving only on what the local sea life brought to him.

Aquaman was called by Deep Blue and the Sea Devils to investigate a seemingly bottomless trench. Deep Blue was dragged into the trench by a bizarre sea creature. Aquaman went into the pit, but the Dolphin could not follow all the way because of the pressure. Aquaman encountered Black Manta, now a horrible manta ray-like monster thanks to his deal with Neron. The two arch-enemies battled at the bottom of the trench, encountering the ghost of a dead leviathan. The creature "consumed" Black Manta and tried to do the same thing to Aquaman, but Arthur used the full brunt of his psychic abilities to drive it off. Fleeing back up the trench, he retrieved Deep Blue and they returned to the Sea Devils' sub. Up from the trench, Arthur discovered that his flesh was now covered in scales. Getting called back to Poseidonis, Aquaman found the Shark had taken up residence. A mental battle ensued and Aquaman forced the Shark to devolve back into a normal tiger shark. Arthur had now mutated further - hard green scales and a webbed hand, looking just like Kordax. The sea life began to fear him. Aquaman went on a quest of soul searching, reflecting on the fact that his inner fears were that he is a failure as a king and as a man. A minor journey through time thanks to Atlan helped him put things in perspective. Returning to the present, Aquaman's appearance was restored (although his hand was still webbed).

### Triton

Seeking to regain the trust of the sea life, Aquaman appealed to Poseidon who forced Aquaman to battle his son, Triton. Aquaman won the fight, and Poseidon restored his title as "King of the Seas." A spiteful Poseidon blinded Aquaman in his bitterness. One short adventure with Animal Man later (one that involved Animal Man teaching Aquaman to expand his powers some more), Arthur's sight was restored. The people of Poseidonis, forced by Aquaman to serve the people of Tritonis whom they maimed, were finally released of their service and return to their city. Aquaman greeted them and they accepted him as their king once more (while marveling at the state of their city, now resting on the ocean's surface). Vulko encouraged thoughts of Koryak that drifted towards a coup de tat. The Dolphin, having lost all interest in Arthur, now entered into a relationship with Tempest. Doctor Polaris invaded the city, using his vast power to turn it on its side. The battle was only won with the help of Maxima, who had come to make Arthur her new king. Aquaman was not interested, even if she was found appealing with the citizens of Poseidonis. When the Millennium Giants emerged, Aquaman was unable to stop one from destroying Thiena Na Oge. Meanwhile, Triton - anger over his loss to Aquaman and the contempt his father has for him - killed Poseidon and seized most of his power. He approached the Mother of Monsters for aid against Aquaman. Triton attacked Atlan first, but the sorcerer managed to get a warning to Poseidonis. Triton attacked the city next, destroying the sentient starship completely and returning the city to the ocean floor. Aquaman was stabbed in the battle, and his soul traveled to the Greek Underworld. He managed to fight his way in to see Hades and convinced Hades to allow him and Poseidon back to the land of the living. Poseidon took back his power from Triton before killing his son and casting his trident at Aquaman's feet.

### Oceanid

A.J. reappeared as an amnesiac believing himself to be Aquaman, and fought Orin for supremacy in Atlantis until his mind was restored using the Clear. They returned to his home dimension Oceanid where Mera led a resistance against alien invaders called the Maritorn. Aquaman used his ability to communicate with the oceans and united the entire planet's fighting forces with Naiad's help. Their coordinated attack quickly brought the Maritorn to their knees, and he personally defeated their leader, Lord Nemo. Orin and Mera returned to their home dimension, while A.J. stayed behind and became the Aquaman of Oceanid.

Koryak attempted to kill his father and seize control of Atlantis, but he was easily stopped and Arthur had him banished.

### The Lurkers

Aquaman declared Poseidonis would be permanently renamed Atlantis. They were attacked by the Lurkers for encroaching on their territory, and their leader Noble destroyed Orin's hook hand during a fight. He began using a cybernetic replacement hand. Using superior tactics to defeat the more powerful invaders, the Lurkers were subjugated and Noble was appointed as an Atlantean dignitary. Noble began to romantically pursue Mera during a tour of the Hidden City, assisting in her rescue from the Lava Lord of the Fire Trolls.

Garth got the Dolphin pregnant and subsequently married her. Arthur reconciled with Mera.

### The Cerdian War

There was a war between Atlantis and the island nation of Cerdia. This war would result in the utter devastation of both Poseidonis and Tritonis as well as ravaging Cerdia as well. It was revealed during the course of the war that the Ocean Master was behind Cerdia's aggression. He also kidnapped Tempest and the Dolphin's son right after the boy was born. The war was eventually won, the Ocean Master was driven off, and Cerdia was annexed by Atlantis. As a token of peace, Tempest and the Dolphin named their son Cerdian (nicknamed "Ian").

### Our Worlds at War

Main article: Our Worlds at War

Atlantis had not fully recovered from the war with Cerdia when it was drawn into another conflict. This one involved the coming of Imperiex, a force of nature that was coming to destroy the planet. An intergalactic alliance of aliens came together to make their stand on Earth. The Justice League of America's big guns were quickly swept aside by Imperiex's advance force. Aquaman gave his all against one of the probes, battling it over Poseidonis. An explosion resulted and when the water cleared, Atlantis was gone and a great rift in the ocean remained.

### The Obsidian Age

Main article: JLA: The Obsidian Age

Following its disappearance, the Justice League discovered the ruins of Atlantis in the modern day on the ocean's surface. Reaching the conclusion that it was sent into the past, they unearthed a graven distress signal from Aquaman. Tempest cast a spell to send them all backwards through time on a rescue mission. Batman's emergency protocols recruited a replacement League in the present day led by Nightwing. The original League traveled backwards 3,000 years to find Aquaman imprisoned while the city was ruled by the League of Ancients. Their leader, Gamemnae, was revealed to have enslaved the "future" Atlanteans upon their arrival for 15 years, trapping Orin's consciousness inside a pool and using their labor to raise the city. Ruthless and prepared for them, the Ancients slaughtered the League members in battle. Manitou Raven was the only one of her followers to recognize Gamemnae's treachery, and set a plan in motion before she could murder all that stand in her way. Through extremely complicated mysticism and time travel, the Justice League made their final stand against Gamemnae. She resurfaceed in the present and tried to enslave humanity through a worldwide drought. Zatanna led a team into the past, and they channeled Aquaman's water wraith form into the sea, where he was able to control all the world's oceans. Using this new immense power, he destroyed Gamemnae's works and sank Atlantis back into the sea. The present-day Atlanteans were brought back to their own time, the Leaguers were reanimated and resurrected, history was corrected and things were set straight. Mera announced that there must still be a reckoning for the destruction and 15 years of slavery; Orin was put on trial by his own people for high treason. Despite the opportunity to avoid punishment through the League's intervention, he chose to accept this fate.

### The Waterbearer

Main article: Aquaman: The Waterbearer (Collected)

His people condemned him to death by desiccation, to be carried out by Rodunn on Traitor's Reef. Arthur barely escaped on the verge of death and found the Secret Sea, where the Lady of the Lake named him the Waterbearer and gave him a new magical hand. Exiled from the ocean, he began working in Ireland at the Mizen Head Lighthouse alongside two locals named McCaffrey and Sweeney. Mera laid comatose and Atlantis had been taken over by a ruling class of despotic sorcerers trained in the Obsidian Age, led by the villainous Hagen. Rodunn attacked Orin on dry land using a battle-suit, but he returned to the side of good when his life was spared. Deciding that it was time for a change in his appearance, Aquaman returned to his classic clean-shaven look. Tempest convinced him to return and fight for his people's freedom, but the Lady of the Lake revealed that there was a dire price for using the hand in anger. This released the Thirst, imprisoned by Merlin, a dark reflection of the Waterbearer who drank life force through the Secret Sea itself.

Vulko began trying to take down the corrupt system from within, assassinating members of the regime using a mutated jellyfish ally called the Man o' War. Aquaman and Tempest traversed the Heavenspan to gain better control of his temperament, while his evil counterpart gained strength by absorbing river spirits, starting with the Elbe. They finally met in the Rhine and did battle against his zombie pirates alongside the champions of Valhalla. Black Manta interrupted when he learned of Aquaman's return, and facilitated the Thirst's escape during an attack. Using his new curative powers, Arthur healed Manta's mental illness and brought him back to sanity. They teamed up with Manta and followed the Thirst into the Dreamtime in Australia while Tempest stayed behind in Atlantis. Moon River fell next to the Thirst's desire. Their final confrontation took place at the Tigris River's Fountainhead, source of the Secret Sea and all Earth's spiritual vitality. He finally defeated the Thirst, but Manta betrayed him and the Fountainhead was stopped anyway. Enraged, Aquaman used his Waterbearer hand against the Thirst in anger and they fused together. Hagen uncovered Vulko's treachery, but released his untold monsters before he could be killed. The Thirst finally reached the Lady of the Lake by controlling their shared form, but Aquaman finally destroyed him by surrendering and defeating the dark side of his own personality. This replenished the Waters of Life, and their energy undid Hagen's dark sorcery as well. Having gotten in touch with his anger, he began to show more compassion to the people he rescued and took time out to help civilians deal with their personal losses.

### Sub Diego

Main article: Aquaman: American Tidal

San Diego was submerged underwater in a massive tragedy that wiped out the entire city along the coast. The first person rescued was a girl with gills named Lorena Marquez, who led them to believe that there might be more survivors. In his public address to the water-breathing refugees, he let them know that they would never be able to leave.

Their investigation of the disaster finally led them to their culprit, the scientist Anton Geist. Geist revealed that he introduced a genetic mutation into the water supply, causing San Diego's populace to develop gills when submerged in salt-water; he believed that climate change would eventually lead to a global apocalypse, and that he was saving humanity. Lorena beat him for murdering her parents, and Aquaman realized that he must have been working for someone else. Geist was forced to stay below and help the people he had condemned, or else face retribution from the company he worked for on the surface. Aquaman became the self-declared leader of Sub Diego.

Their first super-villain was a telekinetic man calling himself the Eel who attempted to take over their local organized crime syndicate. Batman's power dampeners broke in custody, and Aquaman had to fight him on his own terms by telepathically controlling microscopic organisms in the villain's lungs. Investigating a downed naval base with the Sea Devils, Aquaman met a new cybernetic Marauder attempting to steal military secrets and sell them to the highest bidder. The new Devils' team, led by Dane Dorrance and including David Davies, Gideon Rivers, Kooch Kucharski, Melinda Spyros and Sigourney Amundsen, helped him take the malevolent robot down. They also began to experience an influx of designer drugs when a criminal named Ned Moriarty began smuggling in cocaine and heroin, forcing Aquaman to take him down.

The Ocean Master used sorcery to switch lives with his brother during Retroverse, creating a world where Aquaman was synonymous with tyranny and the Ocean Master was an undersea freedom fighter. Lorena Marquez was given a new Aquagirl costume as his second-in-command.

Geist was the only person left unaffected, and he helped Orin reawaken to his true identity. Orin undid the magic, necromancy using the bones from his dead hand, and restored things to the way they should have been.

Alonzo Malrey, a police officer who willingly subjected himself to Geist's genetic mutations, became the city's next resident protector. Reporter Esther Maris became a new ally in the media. The evil corporation that sank Sub Diego, Progene Tech, announced publicly that they patented the water-breathing genetic mutation strand... effectively copyrighting Aquaman's DNA. Vulko returned as ambassador of Atlantis and asked him to become their King again, but he refused. A serial killer murdered eight people, forcing Aquaman to enlist the help of former homicide detective Chandra Abbott and the Scarecrow as a profiler. Abbott was revealed to be the murderer, although her actions indirectly led to the restoration of the police department. Aquagirl allied with Koryak in Atlantis, where they were refusing to grant Sub Diego's residents citizenship. Black Manta began to work with Progene in both their best interests. Aquaman began a romantic relationship with Esther Maris. Manta arrived in Sub Diego and began gaining approval by preaching his racial rhetoric to the inhabitants. Arthur learned from Koryak during their reunion that Mera was sick, but no longer in a coma. He fought through Atlantean border defenses to see her again, missing a date with Esther in the process. Hagen's sorcery had caused Mera to develop lungs, and Atlantean sorcery was the only thing keeping her alive. Tempest and Aquaman had a knock-down, drag-out brawl over whether or not Mera should be able to leave, which Arthur won decisively. She was taken to Geist for treatment, but it was revealed that Geist was an O.M.A.C. and he was activated during The OMAC Project. Koryak managed to destroy the OMAC during their battle, leading to Geist's death as the host body. Manta underwent a surgical procedure to give himself gills. In an effort to undo Hagen's sorcery and save Mera, Tempest gathered the most powerful Atlantean mages. This attracted the attention of the Spectre during his Day of Vengeance rampage to wipe out all magic.

### Infinite Crisis

Main article: Infinite Crisis

During Villains United, Aquaman's allies defended Sub Diego against an attack by the Secret Society of Super-Villains. Tempest and Hagen teamed up to fight the Spectre, but he destroyed Atlantis with a single blow. Atlantean refugees were forced to move to Sub Diego in the crisis. Arthur grieved; Vulko and Koryak were found dead in the wreckage, with Tempest having gone missing. Aquaman was later seen during the Battle of Metropolis fighting Bane and the Headhunter. Manta decided to abandon his political plans for Sub Diego, and simply strike while his nemesis was at his weakest. Captain Malrey was injured in the attack, and Aquaman finally lost it. He destroyed Manta's ship and left him to die in a feeding frenzy of sharks.

The Elongated Man met him in the ruins of Atlantis during 52, where he had gone insane with tragedy and became a delusional, rambling hermit. Trying to restore San Diego and save its inhabitants at the behest of Aquagirl, he made a pact with the Sea Gods for incredible power. In exchange, he became the vessel for something "strange, dark and terrible" as his mind and body were warped into a new form.

### The Dweller in the Depths

Main article: Aquaman: Sword of Atlantis Vol 1

Orin became the Dweller in the Depths, a monstrous figure with great magical power at the expense of his mind. He met Arthur Joseph Curry, a relative of Tom Curry who gained similar powers through his own father's genetic experimentation. Falsely remembering his own life story as a prophecy, he encouraged the young man to become Aquaman with King Shark and himself as companions. They met Mera attempting to re-establish Atlantean society on their travels, but the Dweller could not face her. Vulko's ghost was discovered when Arthur Joseph met the Sea Devils at Windward Home, an international think-tank and safe haven run by James Lockhart and Elsa Magnusson. Lockhart informed him that his father was found dead, and Vulko insisted that his destiny was to reunite the seas and rebuild Atlantis. The Ocean Master was discovered to be responsible for enslaving the Atlantean people, and they launched a rescue mission. During the attack, the Dweller started to question his "prophecy" when he met his wife and brother. Mera showed him his true identity when she realized what had happened to him, but he refused to be swayed from his path. He was also shown to be half-water now, instead of just his hand. Arthur Joseph Curry finally accepted his destiny after they defeated the Ocean Master's army, and Orin officially knighted him the new Aquaman.

Tensions built between Orin and King Shark due to broken memories of their first encounter.

The Dweller attempted to teach Arthur Joseph about heroism, having them travel the Atlantic Ocean, bringing order to chaos. The Fisherman, in a more brutal form, captured them, although the underestimated Aquaman defeated him and rescued the local people.

The Order of the Thorny Crown believed that Orin was the only one who could stop their God's rise to power, and the Sea Bishop ordered his death. His agent, the Pardoner, constructed an undead corpse assassin named Narwhal, implied to be Orin's son, and set him loose. The remaining Atlantean army tried to stop Narwhal from entering the gates of Atlantis, but they were unsuccessful and he murdered the Dweller after defeating Aquaman in combat. The JLA came to pay their respects during the funeral, but expressed concern over whether or not he could eventually revive in his new mystical form. His body decomposed into water.

The Lady of the Lake eventually revealed that Orin shared a deeper connection with Arthur Joseph than previously thought. Sharing both his DNA and stolen life water, Arthur Joseph received a portion of Orin's soul in the spell that raised Sub Diego; this led to the Dweller's transformation. Taking the name Joseph Curry, the new Aquaman swore that he would live up to his predecessor's name.

### Blackest Night

Main article: Blackest Night

Orin was revived as one of the Black Lanterns whereby his hand has been restored to his corpse. Aquaman was among the 12 people revived by the White Lantern Central Power Battery at the dawning of Brightest Day.

### Brightest Day

Main article: Brightest Day

Aquaman reunited with Mera in Amnesty Bay. During an attack on Somalian pirates, he discoverd that he has retained his Black Lantern ability to command undead sealife. Mera's twin sister, the Siren, returned from their dimension leading a Death Squad. They attacked them in the Bermuda Triangle, and Mera confessed that she was originally sent to Atlantis to murder Arthur. Her home dimension of Xebel was revealed to have actually been a penal colony holding separatists who waged war against Atlantis. The Entity appeared again when Deadman gained control of the White Lantern, and his voice commanded Aquaman to find Jackson Hyde. Mera revealed that she already knew the child, and that he was Black Manta's son. Arthur stopped Manta and the Siren from murdering the boy's adoptive father after he charged Mera with finding someone to help protect the surface world from her people.

They escaped together with Mr. Hyde and discovered that part of Jackson's birthright was a map. This led them to an ancient suit of battle armor with Waterbearers to control his powers; Aquaman compelled Jackson to follow his destiny and help imprison the armies of Xebel. They made their stand at the beginning of the Aquawar when the Siren led hundreds of Xebel soldiers to march against surface dwellers on the beaches. Black Manta appeared again and chopped his hand off with a butcher knife. Jackson cauterized his wound, and Mera pushed her hard water powers past their limits to destroy the enemy force in a massive tidal wave. Underwater, they were decimated by Aquaman's army of undead sea creatures, and Jackson used his new powers to turn the barrier back on, imprisoning Manta and the colony of Xebel in the Bermuda Triangle again. His mission completed, the Entity restored Arthur's life back to him completely and then vaporized him. He was converted into one of the four Elementals representing water, alongside Firestorm representing fire, Hawkman and Hawkgirl representing wind, and the Martian Manhunter representing earth. The Entity explained that his greater purpose was to bring them together so they could protect humanity from the Dark Avatar, a version of the Swamp Thing whose connection to the Green had been tainted by Nekron. Their presence staved him off long enough to resurrect Alec Holland. Holland was transformed into the new Swamp Thing and destroyed his evil counterpart, becoming the new avatar of the White Lantern Corps. Aquaman was returned to normal with his hand repaired. Re-examining the weapons that Xebel used when he returned, he realized that they were Atlantean in origin.

- Wonder Woman is an unrequited love interest. Aquaman has demonstrated feelings for her, and once accidentally confessed them while gripping her magic lasso during a team-up. ## Powers and Abilities

### Powers

Atlantean Physiology: The Atlanteans are an offshoot of humanity that is biologically adapted to the deep ocean environment. For most Atlanteans, this means that they can breathe underwater, endure the pressures of the deep (among other adaptations), and can only spend a very limited time out of water. He also possesses superhuman levels of strength, speed, endurance and durability, all by-products of his body being adapted to survive unprotected in the tremendous pressures of the ocean depths. Although other Atlanteans possess similar adaptations, Aquaman's royal lineage makes him a super-Atlantean as well as super-human, and as such he has greater physical capabilities than does a typical Atlantean.

- Amphibious Nature: Aquaman, as an Atlantean, can breath underwater and on land. He can be immersed indefinitely without suffering any ill effects but being super-Atlantean due to his royal lineage, he can survive on land longer than an average Atlantean. This length can be extended by the humidity in the atmosphere but eventually prolonged dehydration will weaken him thus he must return to aquatic environment at various period.

- Enhanced Vision: His ability to see in the murky depths of the ocean allows him exceptional night vision while on land. He has claimed that his eyes are adapted to see at 6,000 fathoms (or 36,000 feet below the surface of the water).

- Enhanced Hearing: several times more acute than human capacity.

- Enhanced Smell: several times more acute than human capacity.

- Superhuman Strength

- Superhuman Stamina

Superhuman Durability

- Energy-Heat Resistance: Aquaman is extremely resistant to energy/heat based attacks as he has with stood multiple blasts from Amazo's heat vision, Black Manta's heat beams, and energy blasts from an OMAC.

- Superhuman Reflexes: While no speedster, Aquaman's reflexes are superior to even near metahumans such as Deathstroke the Terminator, and his reflexes are on the order of 12-15 times faster than a normal human. Aquaman is probably the greatest swimmer in the world.

- Telekinesis: Orin has an undefined level of telekinetic abilities that has been very rarely touched upon.

Marine Telepathy: Aquaman has the ability to communicate directly with sealife and has done so throughout most of his life. Lately he possesses a greater range of control that can actually force sea creatures to obey his whims. Currently, he requests their assistance and they comply. He has chosen to continue to request their assistance since, if he forces them to comply, they will ultimately lose all of their respect for him. He now also has access to a quantum field phenomenon called "The Clear". "The Clear" quantum phenomenon has been observed with the subject called the Swamp Thing who called it the Green or the Morphogenic Field of Animal Man (also called the Red). Aquaman is also an accomplished telepath in general and can perform such telepathic feats as mind to mind communication, mind probes, mental domination of a non-aquatic mind, and mental attacks. The potency of such mental domination is unclear, but he has been seen to use his powers to great effect against the White Martians and Doctor Polaris.

- Psychic Vibration Manipulation: Aquaman learned a new technique while traversing the home planet of the Hexapuses, he could channel his psionic force into potent mind and body disorienting psychic waves which can throw his opponents off-balance as though they were being jostled about by a tremendous earthquake.

- Organism-Artificial Intelligence Synergy: (Formerly) Once when Orin's city of Poseidonous sat perched on top of the asteroid ship Ramona and he would develop a psychic rapport with the A.I. operating the ship. Aquaman could essentially, even before meeting with its consciousness, mentally command the advanced technologies of the space vessel to operate almost everything that was automated in Atlantis itself. Around and after he deepened his bond with the synthetic entity within it, Arthur could willfully command his mobile fortress to go wherever he wanted it to go and strike out at whatever he focused his mind on.

- Limited Mentifery: (Possibly Formerly) To a marginal extent, Orin is able to use his marine telepathy to alter the fabric of reality on a microcosmic level; I.e., controlling and manipulating the evolutionary phases and/or physical transformation of others through the power of his mind alone, having done so against an ancient primordial entity as old as the Earth itself sitting at the bottom of one of the planet's deepest trenches, and then again against the villain known as the The Shark whom he mentally willed to change back into an ordinary tiger shark instead of the hyper-evolved entity he initially was. This has the debilitating side effect of kickstarting an atavistic transformation which causes him to grow sharpened teeth, green scales and webbing along his hands and fingers, accompanied by burning red eyes similar to his ancestor, Kordax; it also disrupts his psionic rapport with sea creatures, as they become frightened by his appearance, and gives him a harsher mindset when using his powers to their fullest.

- Access to the Clear: Aquaman has the ability to sense the primal emotions of aquatic creatures through "The Clear". The Clear seems to function as a universal consciousness of all sealife. Via it, he can communicate or command sea life, even on the other side of the planet. He can see what they see and much more that he is only beginning to realize. He can also push his telepathic powers to reach out to the higher land creatures to find that element of their brains that was once an aquatic dwelling creature. This may account for Aquaman's facility with language.

- Undead Calling (Formerly): Due to having been a Black Lantern when he was first reanimated, Aquaman later retained his ability to call forth undead sea life to do his bidding.

Magic (Formerly): His mutation into the Dweller in the Depths through a bargain with ancient sea gods gave him untold amounts of powerful ancient magics.

Water Emulation (Formerly): Due to either a spell cast by powerful dark wizards and sorcerers, a union between him and a malignant vampiric entity, or further alteration by an outside force, Arthur has had his entire being transformed into that of an elemental affiliated with The Clear, the quantum phenomena connected to waters and all life living within it. In this state of being, Aquaman was an oceanic embodiment of tremendous power capable of assimilating with and manipulating all of the world's oceans at once.

- Ocean Embodiment: Has the all-encompassing ability to control water as a whole within his vicinity and beyond.

- Metamorphosis: Being composed entirely of water, Orin no longer has the physical constraints of his limited physical body.

- Life Force Draining: After his union with the Thirst, effectively becoming the Waterbearer itself, the amalgamated entity that he became could drain all the life-giving water from the world at will.
